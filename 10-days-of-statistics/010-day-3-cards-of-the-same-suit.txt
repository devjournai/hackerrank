PROBLEM:
----------------------------------
You draw 2 cards from a standard 52-card deck
WITHOUT replacing the first card.

Find the probability that both cards are of the SAME SUIT.

STEP 1: UNDERSTAND THE DECK
----------------------------------
A standard deck has:
- 52 total cards
- 4 suits
- 13 cards per suit

STEP 2: FIRST CARD DRAW
----------------------------------
The first card can be any card.
So its probability is:

P(First card) = 52 / 52 = 1

STEP 3: SECOND CARD DRAW
----------------------------------
After drawing the first card:
- Cards remaining = 51
- Cards of the same suit remaining = 12

So,
P(Second card is same suit | First card) = 12 / 51

STEP 4: TOTAL PROBABILITY
----------------------------------
Probability that both cards are of the same suit:

P = 1 × (12 / 51)
P = 12 / 51

FINAL ANSWER:
----------------------------------
Probability = 12 / 51
