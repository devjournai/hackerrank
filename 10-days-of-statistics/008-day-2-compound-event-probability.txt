PROBLEM:
----------------------------------
There are three urns: X, Y, and Z.

Urn X contains:
- 4 red balls
- 3 black balls
Total balls = 7

Urn Y contains:
- 5 red balls
- 4 black balls
Total balls = 9

Urn Z contains:
- 4 red balls
- 4 black balls
Total balls = 8

One ball is drawn from each urn.

Find the probability that exactly 2 of the 3 balls drawn are red
and exactly 1 ball is black.

STEP 1: PROBABILITIES FOR EACH URN
----------------------------------
Urn X:
P(Red)   = 4/7
P(Black) = 3/7

Urn Y:
P(Red)   = 5/9
P(Black) = 4/9

Urn Z:
P(Red)   = 4/8 = 1/2
P(Black) = 4/8 = 1/2


STEP 2: POSSIBLE VALID CASES
---------------------------
(Exactly 2 red balls and 1 black ball)

Case 1:
X = Black, Y = Red, Z = Red
P = (3/7) × (5/9) × (1/2) = 15/126

Case 2:
X = Red, Y = Black, Z = Red
P = (4/7) × (4/9) × (1/2) = 16/126

Case 3:
X = Red, Y = Red, Z = Black
P = (4/7) × (5/9) × (1/2) = 20/126

STEP 3: ADD ALL VALID PROBABILITIES
----------------------------------
Total Probability
= 15/126 + 16/126 + 20/126 = 51/126

Simplify:
51/126 = 17/42

FINAL ANSWER:
----------------------------------
Probability = 17/42
