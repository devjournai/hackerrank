PROBLEM:
--------
In a single toss of 2 fair six-sided dice, find the probability that:
1 The two dice show different values, and
2 The sum of the two dice is exactly 6


STEP 1: TOTAL POSSIBLE OUTCOMES
------------------------------
Each die has 6 faces.

Total possible outcomes = 6 × 6 = 36


STEP 2: OUTCOMES WHERE THE SUM IS 6
----------------------------------
Possible ordered pairs (Die 1, Die 2):

(1, 5)
(2, 4)
(3, 3)
(4, 2)
(5, 1)

Total outcomes with sum = 6 → 5


STEP 3: OUTCOMES WHERE VALUES ARE DIFFERENT
------------------------------------------
The pair (3, 3) must be excluded because both dice show the same value.

Valid favorable outcomes:

(1, 5)
(2, 4)
(4, 2)
(5, 1)

Total favorable outcomes = 4


STEP 4: CALCULATE PROBABILITY
-----------------------------
Probability
= Favorable outcomes / Total outcomes
= 4 / 36
= 1 / 9


FINAL ANSWER:
-------------
Probability = 1 / 9
