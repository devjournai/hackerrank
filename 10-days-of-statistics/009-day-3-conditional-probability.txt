PROBLEM:
----------------------------------
Suppose a family has 2 children.
It is known that at least one of the children is a boy.

Find the probability that both children are boys.

STEP 1: LIST ALL POSSIBLE OUTCOMES
----------------------------------
For two children, the possible gender combinations are:

BB  -> Boy, Boy
BG  -> Boy, Girl
GB  -> Girl, Boy
GG  -> Girl, Girl

STEP 2: APPLY THE GIVEN CONDITION
----------------------------------
We are told that one child is a boy.

So we exclude the case where there are no boys:
GG

Remaining possible cases:
BB
BG
GB

Total valid cases = 3

STEP 3: FAVORABLE OUTCOME
----------------------------------
We want both children to be boys.

Favorable case:
BB

Total favorable cases = 1

STEP 4: CALCULATE PROBABILITY
----------------------------------
Probability
= Favorable cases / Total valid cases
= 1 / 3

FINAL ANSWER:
----------------------------------
Probability = 1 / 3
