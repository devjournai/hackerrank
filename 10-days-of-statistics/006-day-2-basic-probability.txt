PROBLEM:
--------
In a single toss of 2 fair six-sided dice, find the probability that
their sum is at most 9.


STEP 1: TOTAL POSSIBLE OUTCOMES
------------------------------
Each die has 6 faces.

Total possible outcomes = 6 × 6 = 36

STEP 2: FAVORABLE OUTCOMES (SUM ≤ 9)
-----------------------------------
Possible sums range from 2 to 9.

Sum : Number of ways
2   : 1
3   : 2
4   : 3
5   : 4
6   : 5
7   : 6
8   : 5
9   : 4

Total favorable outcomes
= 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 5 + 4 = 30

STEP 3: CALCULATE PROBABILITY
-----------------------------
Probability
= Favorable outcomes / Total outcomes
= 30 / 36
= 5 / 6


FINAL ANSWER:
-------------
Probability = 5 / 6
