PROBLEM:
----------------------------------
A bag contains:
- 3 red marbles
- 4 blue marbles

Two marbles are drawn at random WITHOUT replacement.

Given that the FIRST marble drawn is red,
find the probability that the SECOND marble is blue.

STEP 1: UNDERSTAND THE GIVEN CONDITION
----------------------------------
The first marble drawn is red.

So after the first draw:
- Red marbles remaining = 2
- Blue marbles remaining = 4
- Total marbles remaining = 6

STEP 2: PROBABILITY OF SECOND MARBLE BEING BLUE
----------------------------------
Probability
= Number of blue marbles remaining / Total marbles remaining
= 4 / 6

STEP 3: SIMPLIFY
----------------------------------
4 / 6 = 2 / 3

FINAL ANSWER:
----------------------------------
Probability = 2 / 3
